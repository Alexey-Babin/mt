## 1.1.** Purpose, scope of application and main provisions**

### 1.1.1.The Code of Corporate Ethics (hereinafter referred to as the Code) establishes internal rules applicable to the International Investment Bank Personnel (hereinafter referred to as MIB and Bank).It defines the Bank's corporate values and principles and sets professional and ethical standards, expected from its staff.

### 1.1.2.The provisions of the Code apply to all Bank staff.This Code also applies to:

### Persons working in the MIB as part of an internship program;

### persons providing services to the MIB in accordance with civil law treaties.

### Developing the Code and further integrating it into the Bank's daily activities is one of the most important tasks in the development of corporate culture and enhancing the effectiveness of the work of the Staff, as well as in processes aimed at preventing potential losses and reducing risks.

### 1.1.4. The Code establishes the basic corporate values, rules of conduct, based on high professional and ethical standards, which the activities of the Bank's employees, regardless of their positions, must comply with.

### 1.1.5. The Code establishes general principles, which correspond to the Bank's values such as partnership, efficiency and business reputation. Some rules of the Code, aimed at identifying conflicts of interest, require the examination of situations and the provision of information by the Staff on Close Relatives.

### 1.1.6.The Code aims to introduce common values, which define the unique image of the EIB, into the Bank's corporate life and business relationships with all stakeholders.

### 1.1.7.The Code's tasks include:

- the introduction of corporate values, which will be respected. contribute to the achievement of strategic objectives and mission fulfillment. Bank; and

- establishing standards that will help employees determine their own. behaviour in those areas of daily activity where it may be possible to the potential conflict of interest;

- providing recommendations and explanations regarding interpretation and interpretation. the application of ethical values and principles;

- ensuring a common understanding of the threats and risks to the Bank's operations, arising from the employee's corporate behavior.

### 1.1.8.The Bank welcomes the open discussion of the provisions of the Code by all employees, as well as the cooperation aimed at making changes or additions to the Code.

### 1.1.9. The Code shall be approved by the Board of Directors of the Bank and further revised as necessary (at least once every five years).

This document may only be disclosed to third parties in the in accordance with the Regulatory Documents.
